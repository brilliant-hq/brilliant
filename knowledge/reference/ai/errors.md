# When a turn goes wrong

**When the provider refuses.** A provider error never shows its raw text in the chat: the card names the provider and the cause in plain words with the one next step, for example "Gemini rejected the request (invalid API key). Update the key in Settings to continue." (with an Update API key button, which opens the key editor of the provider the chat runs on, a custom endpoint's included), "Gemini rejected the request (model not available). Pick another model to continue.", or "Gemini rejected the request (conversation too long). Compact to continue."; a key out of credit reads "Your OpenAI account is out of credit. Add credit at OpenAI, or pick another model."; a provider that stayed busy through the retries reads "Anthropic stayed busy through 3 retries. Send again in a minute."; a connection that drops before the reply starts is retried once by itself ("The connection to Anthropic dropped before the reply started. Retrying."). The provider's own message sits behind the card's Details toggle. On the default Brilliant AI lane, when the upstream model stalls or errors while the proxy relays it, the chat shows an in-place waiting row that says so and keeps retrying, for example "Gemini 3.8 Flash is not responding on Google side right now; Brilliant is fine, retrying (5 s)", and once the wait budget runs out it settles to a stop card that says what to do, "Gemini 3.8 Flash is not responding on Google side right now; Brilliant is fine. Send again in a minute.", with a Send again button. A relayed provider error reads "Gemini 3.8 Flash on Google side: busy (429). Retrying in 5 s." while it retries inside that budget; when Google keeps limiting past the budget, the turn ends on the wait it named, for example "Gemini 3.8 Flash on Google side: busy (429). Send again in 28 s." (or "Send again in a minute." for a longer wait). A very large request (2 MB of context or more, usually many images or a long history) that Brilliant AI cannot answer in time ends the turn with "This prompt is 3.2 MB of context, which is more than Brilliant AI can answer in time right now. Resend, or trim the context and resend." and two buttons: Resend (one more try with the same context) and Trim context (compacts the conversation, then resends). Trim context shrinks the context under the 2 MB Brilliant AI answers in time, turning earlier images into thumbnails or removing them, and when that is not possible (the newest message alone is too large) the compact card says why and nothing is resent. A request larger than Brilliant AI takes at all ends the turn with "This prompt is 51.0 MB of context, which is more than Brilliant AI takes in one request. Trim the context and resend." and the Trim context button only. A request that does not finish uploading in time on a slow connection ends the turn with "This prompt is 4.2 MB and did not finish uploading to Brilliant AI on your connection. Send again, or use a smaller image or trim the context." and two buttons, Send again and Trim context; neither is re-sent by itself. When a provider names a wait (a throttle that says how long), the next message holds until that wait has passed, with the pause row counting down, and never goes out early.

**When the request itself goes wrong.** If the model's provider refuses a request because of how Brilliant assembled it (a fault on Brilliant's side, never yours), the chat shows "Something went wrong on our side. Retrying…" and retries the same message once by itself, in the same turn; the answer then continues as if nothing happened. If the retry fails too, the turn ends with "Brilliant AI could not continue this turn. Send your message again.", and sending again starts a fresh attempt. Provider error text is never shown in the chat for these; it goes to the diagnostics log.

**When the chat cannot save to disk.** In the desktop app, each session's transcript is written to disk so it survives a restart. If those writes keep failing (a full disk, a read-only or permission-blocked location, or a synced folder like iCloud or Dropbox holding the file), the chat tells you once, naming the reason in plain words: "Chat history is not being saved to disk (disk full). This conversation stays while the app is open and will not be here after a restart." The conversation still works on screen for now. Freeing space, fixing the folder's permissions, or moving the app's data out of a synced folder lets saving resume. In the web app (browser) the transcript lives in the browser's local storage instead, and when the browser blocks it (private browsing or a full storage quota) the notice is a red tile, "Chat history isn't saving in this browser. Chats are lost when you close this tab." After a few failed saves in a row Brilliant stops retrying and keeps the chats in memory for that tab, and it checks now and then whether saving works again: when it does, the held chats are saved and the tile goes away.

**When the AI names a canvas that does not exist.** A design block aimed at a canvas your project does not have creates that canvas at the path the block names, in the in-app chat exactly as for an outside agent (MCP), and the block builds there, and the new canvas shows in the Files list. A canvas you deleted recently comes back from the trash instead of a new empty one. A path that climbs out of the project (`../`) is refused and nothing is created. When a design block keeps failing the same way, the turn stops with a card like "Line 1 of the last change kept failing the same way, so Gemini 3.8 Flash stopped. Everything before line 1 is on the canvas." and your next message starts fresh.

**Steps the AI handles by itself stay out of the conversation.** A retry after a dropped connection or a fault on Brilliant's side, and pausing the reply to read back the changes show only in the chat's live status line while they happen, and nothing stays in the conversation once they resolve. Only a retry that fails too leaves its card, such as "Brilliant AI could not continue this turn. Send your message again."

**When Brilliant stops the model on purpose.** A turn the harness ends (a model looping on elements that are gone, tool calls that do nothing, a reply budget spent on thinking, or all its tool steps used) ends with one card naming the model and what to do, for example "Gemini 3.8 Flash kept editing elements that are no longer on the canvas (Hero card), so the turn was stopped. Send again to continue." or "Gemini 3.8 Flash made 4 tool calls in a row that did nothing, so the turn was stopped. Send again to continue.". A reply that is cut off mid-way continues by itself, up to three times in one turn (the row reads "Reply cut off; continuing from where it stopped" until it resumes). Cut a fourth time and the turn ends with "The model's stream kept cutting out. Try sending again.". A tool that fails settles its row to the outcome ("Image generation failed: out of credit", or "Image 2 of 3 could not be generated (busy). The other 2 are on the canvas."), with the tool's own message behind Details.

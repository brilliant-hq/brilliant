# Effects

Visual effects that enhance elements with shadows, glows, and blurs. Effects are non-destructive: they wrap or layer over the element's geometry and can be toggled, reordered, or removed without altering the underlying shape.

- `reference/effects/systems`: the two homes, the Effects section and the Fills list
- `reference/effects/liquid-glass`: the Liquid Glass fill, its parameters, tint and presets
- `reference/effects/adding`: adding effects from the inspector and the palette
- `reference/effects/managing`: the effect and fill row controls, tips
- `reference/effects/properties`: drop shadow, outer glow, element blur properties and defaults
- `reference/effects/fill-properties`: inner shadow, inner glow, background blur properties and defaults
- `reference/effects/tokens-order`: token binding, composite shadow tokens, rendering order, export support
- `reference/effects/color-adjust`: the Color Adjust fill, its parameters and presets
